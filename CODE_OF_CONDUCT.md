# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Project discussions should be respectful, constructive, and focused on the
work. Examples of acceptable behavior include:

- giving specific, actionable feedback;
- respecting different experience levels and viewpoints;
- accepting responsibility and correcting mistakes;
- keeping technical disagreements professional.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information, and
deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, and other
content that violates these standards. Repeated or serious violations may
result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. All
reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential where practical.
