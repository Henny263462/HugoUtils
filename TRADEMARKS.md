# HugoUtils Branding Policy

The Apache License 2.0 governs the HugoUtils source code.

That license permits use, modification, forks, and redistribution according to
its terms.

Independent derivative projects may accurately state that they are based on
HugoUtils, contain HugoUtils code, or are forks of HugoUtils.

Examples of acceptable descriptions:

- SuperUtils – based on HugoUtils
- MyMod – using code from HugoUtils

Independent projects should use their own primary name and branding.

Without explicit permission, an independent project must not:

- falsely present itself as the official HugoUtils project;
- falsely claim to be maintained by the official HugoUtils maintainers;
- use the HugoUtils logo or branding in a misleading way that implies official
  status;
- falsely imply endorsement.

This policy does not restrict Apache-2.0 rights to the source code.
